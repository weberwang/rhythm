# Acceptance Checklist

Review the rebuilt Pencil result against the approved preview in this order:

1. `结构`: page framing, section ordering, negative space, alignment
2. `排版`: font family, size contrast, weight rhythm, line length
3. `颜色`: background, surfaces, accents, contrast, semantic highlights
4. `资产`: illustration placement, icon clarity, crop quality, transparency edges
5. `细节`: shadows, radius, texture, separators, decorative balance

## Acceptance Questions

- Does the page hierarchy match the approved preview?
- Are any bitmap slices standing in for text or structural layout?
- Do icons still look crisp at the rendered size?
- Do illustrations preserve the intended role and balance?
- Is the remaining difference intentional and documented?

## Closeout Format

Summarize final review as:

- `已对齐项`
- `剩余差异`
- `是否可接受`
- `下一轮动作`
