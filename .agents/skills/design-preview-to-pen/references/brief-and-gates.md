# Brief And Gates

## Brief Contract

Lock these fields before image generation:

- `目标`: what the screen or poster is trying to communicate
- `页面范围`: one page, one section, one card, or a full flow
- `目标平台`: mobile, desktop, poster, splash, or marketing panel
- `风格关键词`: three to five adjectives
- `插图策略`: no illustration, light illustration, hero illustration
- `图标策略`: line, filled, rounded, geometric, or redraw allowed
- `品牌约束`: palette, logo, forbidden colors, forbidden motifs
- `验收标准`: what must look right for the user to approve

## Discovery Questions

Ask only what is necessary to reduce ambiguity:

1. What is the primary job of this preview?
2. Which parts must be identical in the final Pencil rebuild?
3. Are icons allowed to be redrawn?
4. Should illustrations be regenerated as independent transparent assets?
5. Is the goal exact restoration or structured restoration with minor engineering cleanup?

## Approval Gates

Never cross these gates silently:

1. `需求门`: brief is clear enough to generate preview options
2. `定稿门`: one preview direction is explicitly approved
3. `切片门`: the asset strategy is accepted
4. `Pencil 门`: the app is connected and schema is available
5. `验收门`: the rebuilt result is compared against the approved preview
